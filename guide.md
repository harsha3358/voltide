# Handbook: how VOLTIDE works

> In short: watch the story, do the missions, collect XP, decrypt LAGZILLA's secret message, beat the boss. Learn cloud computing by accident.

## For students

1. Start with **Episode 01 · SPARK**. The others unlock when your facilitator drops the code in class.
2. Every mission (topic) follows the same rhythm:
   - **Volty's hook**: why you should care, in one line.
   - **The idea in one sentence**, then a picture your brain already understands.
   - **New words**, defined like a human would.
   - **Live demo**: try it in the browser. No cloud bill, ever.
   - **Hands-on lab**: commands for *your own* Ubuntu VM, with the expected output shown.
   - **Checkpoint**: one question, **+50 XP**. Three right in a row starts a combo for double XP.
3. Follow the step strip at the top of each mission (**Learn → Try it → Lab → Check**). It ticks as you go; tap a step to jump to it.
4. In labs, press **I ran it** after each command (+10 XP each, +40 when the whole lab is done).
5. At the end, press **Done · Next** (+30 XP). It completes the mission, cracks one fragment of LAGZILLA's **secret transmission** and takes you to the next one. On a phone, the bar at the bottom does the same with ‹ and ›.
6. At the end of each episode: the **boss fight**. 20 seconds per question, 3 lives, +400 XP and a badge.
7. Your **profile** (click the XP bar) has your handle, badges, rank ladder and certificates. Win all four episodes for the **Workshop Graduate** certificate.
8. Stuck on a word? Use the [jargon buster](#/glossary).

## Buttons you'll see

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| XP bar (top) | Opens your profile |
| 🔍 | Glossary / jargon buster |
| 📖 | This handbook |
| 🔈 | Sound effects on/off (off by default) |
| ⚡ | Full effects ↔ lite mode (for older lab PCs) |
| ↑ (bottom-left) | Back to top |
| Copy mission link | Share a mission with your squad |

The 3D scene at the top of each episode is interactive: move your mouse to look around, tap it to make something happen. On slow PCs, press ⚡ for lite mode and it switches off.

Any link marked **↗** opens the real website (VirtualBox, AWS Console, Google App Engine…) in a new tab.

## Starting over

On the profile page, scroll to **Reset**. You get two options:

| Option | Clears | Keeps |
|---|---|---|
| **Reset score** | XP, rank, badges, combos, boss wins, lab ticks | Mission progress, checklists, unlocked episodes |
| **Reset everything** | All of it: XP, badges, missions, checklists, unlocked episodes, team card, handle | Only the lite-mode setting |

Use **Reset everything** on a shared lab PC before the next student sits down.

## The golden rule of the cloud

> Use the **free tier**, set a **billing alert**, and **stop or delete** every cloud resource when you're done. Stop what you start.

See [cloud account safety](#/session/1/topic/cloud-account).
